A little about us

نبذة بسيطة عنا

Oneway is a leading Independent Freight Forwarder based EGYPT and has been established in 2015. oneway helps a wide range of clients from multi-national companies to individuals looking to transport freight around the world. one way is your one-stop for international shipping and logistics needs. At competitive rates, our friendly staff will provide you with reliable, transparent, and personalized service, covering every detail of shipping your goods around the world. With over 8 years of experience as a freight forwarder. oneway provides the know-how to handle any challenge that may arise with your imports or exports so you can focus on what you do best, building your business. one way is a Global Logistics Solutions provider offering a multitude of services to meet your business needs. Our Comprehensive Global Network of Agents enables seamless freight transportation across international markets and induces effective value adding services for customer benefit. ONEWAY moves any cargo on any transport mode and mind-set to Service Excellence. , we provide a first-class, highly capable and adaptable transport management solutions and all kinds of highly value added integrated logistics services to the Small and Middle -Sized Enterprises(SME’S ). We take care of your most valuable goods as if they were our own.

ون واي شركة رائدة في الشحن الدولي والتخليص، مقرها مصر وتأسست عام 2015. نخدم شركات متعددة الجنسيات وأفراداً يرغبون في نقل بضائعهم حول العالم، وبأسعار تنافسية وخدمة موثوقة وشفافة تغطي كل تفاصيل الشحن. بخبرة تزيد عن 8 سنوات نتعامل مع أي تحدٍّ في الاستيراد أو التصدير لتتفرغ أنت لتنمية أعمالك. شبكة عالمية من الوكلاء تتيح نقلاً سلساً للبضائع بأي وسيلة نقل، مع حلول لوجستية متكاملة للشركات الصغيرة والمتوسطة. نهتم ببضائعك كأنها بضائعنا.
